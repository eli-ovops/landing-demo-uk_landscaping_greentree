## Brand Read and dials

**Design Read: Mitcham landscaping · grounded fieldcraft + clear geometry · register=brand · SOUL=7 · SPECTACLE=3 · DENSITY=5 · hero-engine=static photographic composition.**

The lazy industry default is sage/beige, leaf icons and generic service cards. It is rejected for a deep evergreen/slate landscape that reads as practical site work, with warm timber only inside the visual material. Premium substrate: evergreen-tinted near-black, mineral-grey surface, one acid-lime accent, restrained grain, tinted borders, `Manrope` display/body tension and one 14px rounding system.

## Two materially different directions

1. **Field journal** — pale editorial paper, monochrome site photography, quiet process timeline. Rejected: too close to a garden-design studio and too low-contrast for direct local quote intent.
2. **Ground / Line / Living** — deep green field surface, slate construction geometry, one direct utility lime accent, full-bleed original visuals and an off-centre contact-first hero. Selected: it makes the tangible landscape/install relationship legible without pretending the business is a luxury landscape architect.
