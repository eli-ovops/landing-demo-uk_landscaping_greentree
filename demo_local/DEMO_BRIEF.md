# Demo brief — Landscaping Greentree, Mitcham

## Scope and boundary

Local L0 static Concept Demo only: responsive HTML/CSS with an external `tel:` / `mailto:` route. No CMS, payment, form submission, booking, tracking, upload, public link, deployment or customer contact. The shown direction is a complete £99 AS-SHOWN website direction; £199 only permits one later consolidated text/image update. `OFFER / TRANSFER PLAN: NOT RUN` and `FINAL SOURCE / ASSET TRANSFER ELIGIBILITY: NOT RUN`.

Actual Demo Minutes: 7 minutes from first generated demo asset (19:45) through initial desktop/mobile evidence capture (19:52), excluding the already-completed customer research package.

## Audience, first-screen task and fact basis

Audience: a Mitcham homeowner comparing a garden improvement provider, primarily on a phone or search result. First-screen task: establish that this is a local landscaping business and make three useful paths recognisable—outdoor spaces, paving/groundwork and fences/gates—before offering a straightforward phone/email quote route.

Confirmed facts used: Landscaping Greentree; Mitcham; 07538 386948; [REDACTED_EMAIL]; official published categories that include landscaping/garden design, patios/paving, fencing/gates, turfing/planting and driveway work. No hours, owner name, price, review quote, project name, credential, warranty or result claim is used because those facts are conflicting or insufficiently verified.

## Page hierarchy and section jobs

1. Hero — local identity, core promise and contact path beside a concept garden visual.
2. Service trinity — selects a coherent subset rather than reproducing the current mixed catalogue.
3. Work texture — three distinct visuals explain patio/lawn, gates/fencing and driveway/planting; original concept visuals are never described as actual jobs.
4. How an enquiry starts — a three-step factual, static expectation-setting path without inventing a booking system.
5. Bottom 40% — recapture locality, selected capabilities and the direct email/phone quote route.
6. Closing/footer — business identity and exact contact details.

## Non-scope

No self-hosted enquiry form, on-page review claims, live availability, map, booking, WhatsApp claim, artificial customer quotes, project names, staff details, pricing, social proof wall, dynamic animations or transfer-rights assertion.
